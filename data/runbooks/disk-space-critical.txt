RUNBOOK: CRITICAL DISK SPACE
============================

PURPOSE
Prevent service failure when a filesystem or persistent volume approaches capacity.

TRIGGER
- A disk or inode alert fires, writes fail, or a service reports a read-only/full filesystem.

TRIAGE
1. Identify the affected host, mount, volume, service, and remaining capacity. Check whether users or writes are affected.
2. Inspect filesystem and inode usage:

   df -h
   df -i

3. Find large directories on the affected filesystem (GNU/Linux example):

   sudo du -xhd1 <mount-path> 2>/dev/null | sort -h

4. Check for recent log growth, temporary files, backups, container images, or application data. Confirm retention and ownership before touching files.

RESPONSE
1. If logs are growing rapidly, use the service's log-rotation procedure or reduce verbosity through approved configuration. Do not delete active log files by hand.
2. Remove only files confirmed as disposable and covered by the team's retention policy. Never remove unknown application data, database files, or backups to free space.
3. If capacity must be expanded, use the approved volume-resize or storage-provisioning process and check quota and cost impact.
4. If a database or persistent volume is near full, involve its owner before cleanup or resize. Preserve snapshots and backups.

VERIFY RECOVERY
- The affected filesystem has adequate free space and inodes.
- The application can write and its health checks pass.
- Disk usage is stable; the growth source is understood or being tracked.

ESCALATE
Escalate immediately if writes are failing, a database volume is involved, data deletion is being considered, or the cause of growth is unknown. Record the mount, usage, and largest directories without exposing sensitive file contents.
