RUNBOOK: SERVICE UNAVAILABLE
============================

PURPOSE
Restore service when users receive connection failures, timeouts, or HTTP 5xx responses.

TRIGGER
- An availability alert fires or multiple users report that the service cannot be reached.
- Health checks fail or the service's 5xx/error rate rises above its normal baseline.

TRIAGE
1. Record the start time, affected service, environment, regions, and a representative error. Open or update the incident channel.
2. Check whether the issue affects all users or only a region, tenant, endpoint, or dependency.
3. Check the service dashboard, recent alerts, and deploy/change history. Compare error rate, latency, traffic, and dependency health.
4. From an approved host, check the public health endpoint:

   curl -i --max-time 5 https://<service-host>/<health-path>

5. If the service runs on Kubernetes, inspect status and recent events:

   kubectl get pods -n <namespace> -o wide
   kubectl get events -n <namespace> --sort-by=.lastTimestamp

RESPONSE
1. If failures began after a deployment, pause further releases and follow the Deployment Failure runbook. Roll back only through the approved deployment process and with the incident lead's approval.
2. If instances are unhealthy, inspect their logs and readiness/liveness checks. Correct the identified cause before restarting or replacing instances.
3. If a dependency is failing, confirm its impact and use the service's documented fallback or circuit-breaker procedure. Do not repeatedly restart dependent services.
4. If the issue is isolated to one region or instance pool, route traffic away only using the approved traffic-management procedure.

VERIFY RECOVERY
- Health checks pass from outside and inside the service boundary.
- Error rate and latency return to baseline and remain stable for at least 10 minutes.
- A representative user workflow succeeds; the incident lead confirms monitoring is clear.

ESCALATE
Escalate to the service owner and incident lead if the cause is unknown after 15 minutes, data integrity may be affected, or recovery requires a risky traffic or infrastructure change. Preserve relevant logs and timestamps.
