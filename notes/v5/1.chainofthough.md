Shot prompt has disadvantage it cant do Maths,logical reasoning may not work

sequence of thoughts given in the prompt is called chain of thought

Asking model to reason step by step before answer the question

Based on the complexity of the task it is divided into 3 categories

Light Reasoning: Step by step thinking before generating ans , few reasoning steps

Structural Anlysis : Anlyze what can be good, bad , alternative solution before answering
analyze the requirement,evaluate options and recommend

Deep Reasoning: Multi step planning, validation and conclusion
Think all solution , find alternative, validate and provide final answer

COT is a prompting techique where we can encourage the model to provide the intermediate steps(reasoning) before generating the actual o/p , it helps to provide best results and reduce hallucnations
